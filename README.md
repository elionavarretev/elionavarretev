I lead quality strategy for software where mistakes are expensive. Over 10 years in QA and test automation across healthtech, banking, retail and telecom, from building automation frameworks for BCP and Belcorp to owning release readiness for a telehealth platform that serves patients in all 50 U.S. states.

I wrote the book [*Quality Assurance: De Fundamentos a Automatización con IA*](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GRKF9CZD), co-authored 7 peer-reviewed papers in IEEE Xplore, and teach software quality and architecture at UPC (since 2020) and UNI.
